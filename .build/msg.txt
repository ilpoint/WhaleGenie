Drop the permission flags so the page is the only source

--allow-control and --allow-run were a second way to say the same thing the
MCP page says, and two sources for one answer is how a switch ends up with
no effect. The flags are gone: a client is launched with --mcp, and what it
may do always comes from the settings file the page writes.

Checks get a gate of their own (McpGate) instead of writing the switches on
McpControl, which now has no state to write: Allowed and AllowsRun are
questions asked of the page on every call.
